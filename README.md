# 🌍 Global Socio-Economic & Country Comparison Dashboard

An interactive, multi-page **Power BI dashboard** designed to explore global socio-economic conditions, compare countries across key development indicators, and uncover insights into economic performance, public health, and education.

This project transforms international country-level data into meaningful visual insights, helping users understand global development disparities, identify economic trends, and compare nations across multiple dimensions.

## 🔍 Key Analytical Insights

### 1. Executive Overview
- **Global Population:** Analyzes a reported global population of approximately 6 billion people.
- **Global GDP:** Highlights total GDP of approximately $56 trillion and average country GDP of $243 billion.
- **Leading Economies:** Compares major economies, including the United States, China, Japan, and Germany.
- **Development Classification:** Segments countries into development and income groups to reveal global economic disparities.
- **Country Rankings:** Enables interactive comparisons and rankings based on GDP and income indicators.

### 2. Health & Education Analysis
- **Life Expectancy:** Reveals differences in average life expectancy across development groups, with approximately 78.8 years for developed countries compared with 55.3 years for low-income countries.
- **Birth & Death Rates:** Examines demographic patterns across developing countries, including Niger, Uganda, and Afghanistan.
- **Public Health:** Analyzes the distribution of adult HIV/AIDS prevalence, highlighting the reported 46.75% share associated with low-income regions.

### 3. Economic Performance Analysis
- **International Trade:** Compares global imports of approximately $8.819 trillion with exports of approximately $8.820 trillion.
- **Inflation Analysis:** Evaluates price stability across income groups, with low-income countries averaging approximately 9.6% inflation.
- **Economic Growth:** Classifies 211 countries into four growth categories: Rapid Growth, Moderate Growth, Slow Growth, and Economic Decline.
- **Cross-Country Comparison:** Identifies differences in economic performance and highlights disparities between national economies.
